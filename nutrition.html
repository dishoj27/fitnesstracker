Nutrition

Eat balanced meals and get enough protein to support your fitness goals.

💪 How Much Protein Do You Need?

A general fitness range is about 1.2–2.0 g of protein per kg of body weight per day.

Example

If your body weight is 60 kg:

60 × 1.2 = 72 g protein/day

60 × 2.0 = 120 g protein/day

Suggested range: 72–120 g/day
