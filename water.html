Water & Hydration

Keep your body hydrated throughout the day and during workouts.

💧 Daily Water Intake

Water needs depend on body size, activity, climate and food intake.

2.7–3.7 L/day

This is a general adult adequate-intake reference for total water from food and beverages. People who exercise or live in hot conditions may need more.

💧 DAILY ROUTINE

Simple Water Schedule

🌅 Morning

Drink water after waking up and with breakfast.

300–500 ml

☀️ Afternoon

Drink regularly with lunch and between meals.

700–1000 ml

🏋️ Workout

Sip fluids during exercise, especially in hot conditions.

According to thirst & sweat

🌙 Evening

Continue drinking as needed without forcing large amounts.

500–750 ml

⚡ CREATINE

Creatine & Water

How much creatine?

A typical maintenance amount for healthy adults is approximately 3–5 g of creatine monohydrate per day.

3–5 g creatine/day

There is no scientifically established requirement to drink a special extra amount of water because you took creatine.

You can mix your creatine with a normal glass of water and continue your regular hydration throughout the day. Staying adequately hydrated is particularly important when exercising or training in hot conditions.

🥤 SIMPLE ROUTINE

Taking Creatine With Water

Step 1

Measure your usual daily creatine dose.

3–5 g

Step 2

Mix it with a normal glass of water or another suitable drink.

About 250–350 ml is convenient

Step 3

Continue drinking water normally during the rest of the day.

Stay hydrated
⚠️ Important:

The 250–350 ml amount shown here is simply a convenient amount of liquid for mixing a dose; it is not a required "creatine water" target. Your overall fluid needs depend on your body, activity, climate and other factors.

💡 TIPS

Stay Hydrated

💧 Drink Regularly

Spread your fluid intake throughout the day instead of drinking a very large amount at once.

🏃 Exercise

Increase your fluid intake when you sweat more, exercise longer or train in hot weather.

☀️ Hot Weather

Hot and humid conditions can increase fluid losses, so pay attention to thirst and sweating.
